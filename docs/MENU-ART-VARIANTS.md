# Start-menu background studies

Six original illustrations generated using the built-in Image Gen tool. These
are review candidates, not a chosen replacement. Open
[menu-variants.html](../menu-variants.html) through the local development server.
All JPEGs live in `assets/menu/backgrounds/`; PNG originals remain in
`C:/Users/user/.codex/generated_images/01a1d94f-1fa4-7020-8499-5b0fbc485352/`.
JPEG conversion uses System.Drawing, quality 90, original dimensions and colors.
No painting or recoloring was done with code. Scanlines are CSS over the artwork,
below the real menu UI; the image-only view shows the untreated art.

## Cinema references

- [Brother courtyard / passage frame](https://www.uralresort.ru/blog/2013/05/13/blog_2.html): damp masonry, iron gates, puddles and intimate everyday architecture.
- [Brother film frames](https://www.kinoafisha.info/movies/8098609/shots/): old cream-and-burgundy trams, overhead wires and depot atmosphere.
- [Brother 2 gallery](https://www.moviemeter.nl/film/34178): stone ledge, bridge and quiet riverside composition. The generated study relocates the composition to Petersburg; it is not a reproduction of the Chicago setting.
- [Balabanov location interview](https://www.metronews.ru/novosti/peterbourg/reviews/po-ulicam-i-dvoram-rezhissera-balabanova-provedet-ego-syn-2180267/): Petersburg courtyard-well context.

The reference frames were inspected as environment references, not copied into
the project. No film character, actor likeness, poster or movie typography is used.

## Prompt set: first three studies

Shared prompt:

> Create a finished original WIDE 16:9 landscape painted background asset for the Saint City game start menu, approximately 1920x1080. Atmospheric 1990s Saint Petersburg cinema, weathered everyday city, lonely and contemplative mood inspired by urban frames from Aleksei Balabanov's Brother / Brother 2. Cinematic digital matte painting with hand-painted detail, realistic architecture, film-like grain, no glossy tourist postcard feeling. Preserve our existing menu palette: deep plum #24132e shadows, violet #69417f midtones, dusky hot pink #d95098 and lavender sky, a few warm cream window lights. Pink-violet dusk after rain, restrained believable lighting, NO cyberpunk neon signs. Rich city texture but calm readable medium-value zones behind menu UI. Design the center 40 percent to survive a portrait mobile crop. No people, faces, characters, weapons, text, logos, menus, buttons, maps, borders, or scanline overlays; this is solely a background asset. No black letterbox bars.

Variant instructions:

1. **Courtyard:** genuine enclosed five- to six-storey Petersburg court, worn ochre/rose plaster, sash windows, drainpipes, fire ladder, cables, dark arch. Low wide-angle view, wet cobblestones and puddles in lower third, open iron gate at left, one amber lamp, calm violet sky. Damp passage courts from Brother. This first study is called Проходной двор after the owner's clarification that a well must show sky enclosed on all four sides.
2. **Tram:** aged cream/burgundy Soviet tram in far-left third, wet curving rails from bottom center into mauve haze, wires across violet/pink sky, low brick sheds and bare trees. Subdued machines and weeds at edges; upper right calm for map, left dark for title. Brother depot atmosphere.
3. **Embankment:** contemplative stone-ledge/bridge composition from Brother 2 translated to Petersburg, without Chicago towers. Weathered granite parapet diagonally from bottom left, subdued iron bridge, sparse lamps, five-storey opposite-bank buildings in lavender mist, low horizon, broad plum/rose clouds and pink water reflections.

## Review behavior

The comparison page embeds the actual `index.html?menu=1` in a same-origin iframe.
Only that iframe receives the chosen artwork. Desktop uses a 1440 × 900 viewport;
phone uses 390 × 844. Both are scaled to available review space. Candidate links
are retained in the review page hash, and original-image links expose the full
composition. There is no persisted game selection until the owner chooses.
