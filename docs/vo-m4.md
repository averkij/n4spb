## World rules: what became a knock bay / knock record

Method: `lib/fence-bays.js` `knockBays` (+ `splitBays`, `unitPanel`, `unitBox`) for fences, walls, hedges, chains; `lib/knock-furniture.js` `knockFurniture` for benches, bins, lamps and every other free-standing object. Both are used as the Admiralty
ports use them; geometry and colliders sit where they sat (checked: every record's drawn instances are centred on its collider, max offset 0.6 m for objects with asymmetric parts). Each module's `inst.js` got `kBays(K, o)` (a `knockBays` that adds the
triangles of its instances to `K.extraTris`). The Sevkabel culture kit got `knockCaptured` (`culture-kit.js`): the same drawing code is run into a private collector (so the label reservations keep their world positions), moved to the origin of the
record's spot and handed to `knockFurniture`.

* **Smolensky.** `fence.js`: the 2.4 km fence and walls are 682 bays (517 bar fence 2.0 m, 103 concrete wall 2.8 m, 28 plastered wall 2.0 m, 34 cast-iron lot fence on its granite socle 2.5 m), one bay per texture tile, posts stay static. `furniture.js`: 6 lanterns, 48 poles
  (kind lamp), 2 benches, 1 bin. `forest.js`: a shrub (1.4 m) within 14 m of the water has no collider (a bush is not a wall: check 6 of the water test would call it one); trunks (4.5 m) stay solid.
* **Kirova.** `fence.js`: the garden fence, 232 bays (plinth wall, cap, iron panel; posts static; the source used `addFenceBoxes` plain boxes); `lawns.js`: the four clipped hedges, 80 bays; `eryard.js`: the brick walls of the Erarta passage, 18 bays; `gate.js`: the two open gate leaves
  (2 bays) and the six flower bowls (planter records); `furn.js`: 16 benches and 26 park lamps; `erplaza.js`: 6 benches, the parking payment terminal (cabinet), the bike rack (rack, its 3 colliders are one record's items).
* **Sevkabel.** `almshouse.js`: the lattice fence (44 bays); `quarter.js`: the Brusnitsyn yard wall (11 bays); `culture.js`/`fair.js`/`dining.js`: 5 food trailers and 24 fair stalls (kind booth), 3 gallery partitions, the workshop counter with its 5 stools, 2 sofas,
  café / picnic / hall / terrace tables (24), planters (13), the terrace rails (8), print stands (2), the programme board, the coffee counter, the 4 menu stands. `frame.js` `H.cyl` returns its collider.
* **Sea Terminal.** `fence.js` (`fenceRun`, all three kinds): the port mesh fence, the quay closure across the strip to the water edge (a breakable bay, so the quay edge is never closed), 235 bays; `quay.js`: 23 Jersey barriers (kind barrier);
  `boat.js` (not drawn in this area): the bollard chain as bays, the plaque stand as a record. `deck.js`: the thin box of the closed ramp gate (7.4 x 0.5 x 3 m at v -70) is gone: it stood INSIDE the ramp embankment box (53.7 m long, 8 m high), a collider on a collider.

Solid on purpose (structures), by module: the walls of every building (Palace of Culture, Erarta, the hall, the yard blocks, the almshouse, the Skobsky Palace, the terminal, the shed: `addPolyColliders` / `H.wall`), columns and pylons, the gate pavilions and
columns of the garden, the chapel / church plinths and stairs, the church shop (a 6 x 5.5 m building), graves, crosses, steles and memorials (13,067 granite monuments of `gravefield.js`, the Finland Regiment platform, the Shevchenko boulder: no wall, none
flagged by the water audit), the monuments of Kirova / Erarta (Era and Arta, Mir v kreste, Roerich, Sobchak), the playground equipment (swings, slide), the Sevkabel chimneys, the Corten gate plates, the spool towers, the boat, the cable-ring installation and the stage,
the 12 steel columns of the fair, the crane legs, the deck piers / stair block / ramp embankment of the Sea Terminal, tree trunks and thuja.
