# Southern area port: working foundation

The planned map areas are Коломна–Екатерингофский (42) and
Семёновский–Измайловский (45). Their data drafts in `src/areas/kolomna/data/`
and `src/areas/semyonovsky/data/` are generated by
`node tools/prepare-area-port.mjs` from `102_3d_piter`, source revision
`022e72ca8b3a2d0bf716367e7399187cacca019e`. They are **not live areas**.
The script reads the current map footprint and water zones, uses 80 m cells and
a provisional 600 m shore water band, and cuts with the usual 120 m scenery
margin. Verify the water policy and connectivity before adopting these rectangles.

The draft cuts contain 3,215 and 5,172 building records respectively. The map's
source-coverage estimates are 64% and 75% of the two core areas. This is a real
coverage gap, not just missing landmark models: Kolomna's southern rectangles
K35–K50 and Semyonovsky's I48–I53 contain no source data. They need additional
geographic data and authoring before the areas can be presented as complete.

Existing reusable landmarks include the Trinity-Izmailovsky Cathedral,
Technological Institute and Vitebsky Station (`landmarksCentralSouth`), the
Moscow Triumphal Gate (`landmarksMoskovsky` in the source), the Mariinsky,
Nikolsky and New Holland modules, and south-west bridge groups already adapted
in this project. Select groups based on actual territory and data, not whole
source modules blindly. The [city's historical account](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_moscow/information/istor/)
and its [Moscow Triumphal Gate archive](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/c_govcontrol/news/171679/)
are starting references; the [local library's Yekateringof account](https://kircbs.akir.gov.spb.ru/vstrecha-ekateringof-zabyityie-straniczyi-istorii/)
identifies the park on Yekateringof Island. Check maps and visual references for
the actual missing places before authoring them.

Remaining integration: fill geographic gaps; establish and validate final play
rectangles and data, known-building data, area definitions and page metadata;
adapt the relevant landmarks, bridges, water bodies and boat passages; preserve
knockable furniture and breakable water barriers; configure spawns, neighbouring
gates and hand-offs; then run area, road, water, boat, performance and renderer
checks and produce shipping bakes. Do not expose the drafts as playable before
those steps are complete.
