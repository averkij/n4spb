# Yekateringof Park: rotunda and Molvinskaya Column

The area cut maps the open rotunda as building `w96846565`, centred at
(-4834.6, 3203.9); its OSM outline is approximately 11 m across. The landmark
replaces that generic building with a low round platform, eight square piers,
a circular entablature and a shallow grey metal dome. Its height and details are
visual estimates from [this pond-side photograph](https://4traveler.me/travel/sankt-peterburg/park-ekateringof)
and [this closer photograph](https://www.liveinternet.ru/users/3109898/post343651363/),
not surveyed measurements. Spaces between the piers are open, with colliders on
the piers only. The large mapped pond alongside it is `r2306262`.

The Molvinskaya Column is OSM `n1347500198`, at (-5215.8, 3531.9). The
[Narvsky municipal history](https://www.narvski-okrug.spb.ru/map/info/zabytye-dachi/park-ekateringof/)
describes an approximately six-metre red granite column; a
[photograph](https://fotokto.ru/photo/view/6727774.html) shows the square plinth,
shaft and raised base. The scene retains the mapped position and gives the
column and plinth a compact solid collider.

The module claims only these two objects. The original park relation `r2294342`,
the mapped ponds, ways, trees, benches, bins and fences remain available to the
area's existing systems. In particular, the module adds no shoreline walls or
unbreakable water barriers. The long-lost Yekateringof Palace is not rebuilt;
the park's present pond and surviving pavilion are the relevant references.
