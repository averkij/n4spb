# Московская застава: landmarks and neighbourhood identity

The area follows planning map district 6, including its overlap streets. Its
historical municipal name does not mean that the scene should stop at the gate.
The Moskovsky avenue, Victory Park, Chesme ensemble and four Strugatsky gardens
form the recognizable southern sequence. Data uses the fixed city origin.

## Primary references inspected, 2026-11-01

* [District history](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_moscow/information/istor/):
  the gate and the avenue's prewar and postwar architectural sequence.
* [Strugatsky Square naming](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_moscow/news/52160/):
  the junction of Moskovsky, Frunze and Pobedy, near Boris's home on Pobedy 4.
* [Boris Strugatsky memorial plaque](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_moscow/news/255781/).
* [NLR's building history](https://nlr.ru/history_nlr/RA6714/novoe-zdanie):
  Shcherbin's reading and storage complex, the five-storey public wing and
  nine-storey storage. The photographic circular entrance and its recessed
  glazing are the primary modelling reference.
* [NLR architecture](https://nlr.ru/history_nlr/RA3112/arkhitektura).
* [House with the Spire](https://visit-petersburg.ru/leisure/places/tp-201778-dom-so-shpilem/):
  the city tourism board's aerial photograph was downloaded and visually
  inspected. It shows the square open lantern with four columns per face,
  sloped metal roof, long needle, wreath, corner sculpture and surrounding
  six-storey avenue wings.
* [District council protection dossier](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/c_govcontrol/news/306750/):
  Fomin, Daugul and Serebrovsky's constructivist administration building.
* [Library entrance photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Russian_National_Bibliotheque,_Saint_Petersburg.jpg),
  visually inspected: pale stone drum, tall rectangular supports, continuous
  dark clerestory, long horizontal title, recessed glazed wall and low plaza.

Reference photographs remain outside tracked assets; they are modelling
references, not textures. The park has its own specialist research dossier.

## Survey and interpretation

| Site | Source record | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| House with the Spire | w32272976, w231099741, w305161889 | Exact 17 m square base and 76 m mapped tip; open lantern replaces the source's stacked generic prisms. Photo-derived trim and sculptural silhouettes. |
| New National Library | r1806688, r3466512 | Preserve the mapped scalloped footprint and courtyard holes; open the curved front's lower portico and recess its glazed wall. Heights and facade rhythm are photographic estimates, not surveyed elevations. |
| District council | r3483965, r3483966, r3483967 | Preserve the long wing and cylindrical southern chamber. Continuous cornice and repeated narrow windows. Heights are estimated from photographs. |
| Chesme Church | w870663628 | Adapt existing source geometry: pink and white Gothic tracery, pointed windows and five silver lanterns. |
| Chesme Palace | r3100461 and its building parts | Retain exact triangular plan and later wings, corner towers and crenellated roofline from the source. |
| Strugatsky Square | named road chains in city data | Preserve all four gardens and their real dividing carriageways. Add seats and a garden information panel; no speculative statue. |

Generic city generation supplies surrounding blocks and courtyards rather than
duplicating them in landmark geometry. All new building shells retain real
physical walls; open colonnades use separate supports and height-limited
entablature collision. Street furniture uses the shared knock system. Static
facades and silhouettes have different authored LOD ranges; there is no new
per-frame work outside the area's existing simulation.
