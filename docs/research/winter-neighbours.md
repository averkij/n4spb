# Winter around the central districts

The original winter pages are Palace–Liteyny, Smolninskoye and Vladimirsky–Ligovka. For the next three, start with their direct gate neighbours and rank each by distance to the nearest central page. Using the area-weighted centres of the registry's play rectangles, including the shared territory, gives Admiralty–Haymarket (2.09 km), Semyonovsky–Izmailovsky (2.88 km) and Aptekarsky–Posadsky (2.95 km). These distances describe the game map.

## Second group of neighbours

The next three direct neighbours of the original central pages are Finlyandsky (3.22 km), Kronverk–Vvedensky–Petrovsky (3.65 km) and Vasilyevsky–Okrug No. 7 (4.22 km). The same centroid calculation puts Sampson at 4.82 km.

At Finland Station, a November installation photograph places the district tree on Lenin Square. Inside the Peter and Paul Fortress, the city’s holiday programme puts the tree and fair on Cathedral Square. The ice sculpture festival is a separate event at the Naryshkin Bastion. [Station photograph](https://www.fontanka.ru/2024/11/17/74345354/) · [Fortress holiday programme](https://www.spbcult.ru/news/anonsy/petropavlovskaya-krepost-priglashaet-na-novogodnie-programmy/)

On the Spit, the lighting designer’s photographs of the Parade of Planets show spherical lanterns and inclined orbital rings. Their placement frames the Stock Exchange and the Rostral Columns. [Designer’s photo gallery](https://brkno.com/projects/strelka-vasilevskogo-ostrova)

## Third group of neighbours

Sampsoniyevskoye–Lanskoye is the last direct neighbour of the original three central pages (4.82 km). The next ring is two gate crossings from the centre. Its nearest area-weighted play rectangles belong to Kolomna–Yekateringofsky (4.74 km from a central page) and Moskovskaya Zastava (6.00 km). Kolomna connects through Admiralty and Semyonovsky. Moskovskaya Zastava connects through Semyonovsky. These distances rank the game territories and are independent of the walking route.

The Vyborg district lists Akademika Klimova Square among its festive lighting sites. New Holland’s park plan places winter skating on the central lawn. The city’s Moskovskaya Square Christmas programme describes a troika, a sleigh and festive figures inspired by Pushkin’s fairy tales. These three settings give each district its own winter destination. [Vyborg district lighting](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_viborg/news/103750/) · [New Holland park plan](https://www.newhollandsp.ru/images/download/PROJECT_INFO_RUS.pdf) · [Moskovskaya Square fair](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/inspek/news/289361/)

## Palace Square

The city's holiday programme places the sledding hill beside the General Staff building and describes illuminated photo installations on the square. Keep the Alexander Column, the palace frontage and the approach through the General Staff arch legible between installations. [City holiday programme](https://www.gov.spb.ru/press/governor/310107/)

The seasonal post office beside the main tree has a distinctive oversized reading lamp. Its publisher's photo shows the furniture, lettering and contrasting warm light. The city tourism office documents the timber slide and its three lanes. [New Year post office](https://spbdnevnik.ru/news/2025-12-16/novogodnyaya-pochta-peterburgskogo-dnevnika-na-dvortsovoy-ploshchadi-zarabotaet-20-dekabrya) · [Christmas fair](https://visit-petersburg.ru/infovisit/lp/noviy_god/yarmarki/v-peterburge-novogodnee-nastroyenie-sozdast-rozhdesvenskaya-yarmarka/)

## Trinity-Izmailovsky Cathedral

In the street-level photographs the lighter blue metal panels read separately, with staggered rows of gilded stars. Each of the five domes carries a white arched lantern, gilded cupola and cross. The central drum has a deep white colonnade, with windows visible in the intervals. The four smaller domes sit on the arms of the cross-shaped plan. [City tourism photo gallery](https://visit-petersburg.ru/leisure/places/tp-195647-svyato-troitskiy-izmaylovskiy-sobor/) · [City heritage committee restoration account](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/c_govcontrol/news/104072/)
