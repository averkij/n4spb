# Sampson Cathedral and the Vyborg Side in winter

## Sampson Cathedral belfry

The parish describes blind arches in the second tier, an open bell stage, four triangular pediments and an eight-sided tent with blind dormers. The street photograph shows three narrowing rows of small, hooded recesses above the gables. The local model uses those profiles, retains the open passage at street level and puts the little blue onion on its own pale neck. [Parish description](https://sampsony.ru/) · [Street photograph](https://peterburg.center/sites/default/files/sampsonievskiy_sobor_4.jpg)

The museum records a mouth diameter of 2.20 m and an overall height of 2.10 m, including the crown, for the surviving bell cast in 1763. The diocesan photographs of the restored bells show their flared mouths, stepped bronze bands and cast suspension loops. The Blender model gives the historic bell its measured silhouette, hollow interior, clapper and six-loop crown. Five smaller bells show the scale of the restored ensemble. Their placement and relative sizes are an interpretation of the installation photographs. [Museum catalogue note](https://www.cathedral.ru/en/posts/250-let-kolokolu-sampsonievskogo-sobora-2013-06-03) · [Installation photographs by Yuri Torlopov](https://mitropolia.spb.ru/news/parishs/?id=215285)

The Blender source is `tools/model-sampson-belfry.py`. Running Blender in background mode with this script regenerates `src/world/landmarksSampson/belfry-mesh.js`. The cathedral builder places indexed prototypes into its existing near and skyline batches.

## Winter sites

* **Akademika Klimova Square.** The district administration lists the square among its completed New Year installations. A separate design study for this specific square proposes luminous clouds and flying birds in reference to its aviation history. The game adapts those motifs in three freestanding installations. Two illuminated arches and baubles flank the fir on the pedestrian forecourt north of the surveyed fountain basins. The composition and its precise positions are authored for the game. [District announcement](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_viborg/news/103750/) · [Designer's Klimov Square concept](https://www.rkidea.ru/projects/3d-proekt-oformlenie-ploshchadej-sankt-peterburg-9-maya-novyj-god)
* **Lesnaya metro.** The district paper *Vyborgsky Rayon*, issue 23 (224), page 4, 16 December 2024, records a municipal tree near Lesnaya. Its ground-level entrance is at 25 Kantemirovskaya Street. The installation stands west of the mapped pavilion entrance, with the station approach and traffic lanes open. The low baubles and arch follow the winter style of neighbouring districts. [Metro vestibule register](https://metro.spb.ru/uploads/document/adress_vestibule_26.12.2025.pdf) · [Photographs of the approach](https://www.metrowalks.com/ru/petersburg/kirovsko-vyborgskaya/lesnaya)
* **Sampson Garden.** The smaller tree stands beside a surveyed garden path, clear of the chapel, tower passage and cathedral approach. This is a seasonal composition for the game. Its modest scale keeps the church visible across the former cemetery garden. [Peter the Great Institute's survey and photographs](https://www.petersway.org/monuments/russia/saint-petersburg/st_sampson_cathedral/)
