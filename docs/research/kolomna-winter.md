# Winter in Kolomna and Yekateringofsky

## Narva Triumphal Gate

The State Museum of Urban Sculpture gives the overall height as 31 m, the chariot with Victory as 7.6 m and the height of the six horses as 4.5 m. Its inventory identifies the copper cladding and names the sculptors. [Museum inventory](https://gmgs.ru/post/17574) · [Museum photographs](https://gmgs.ru/narva_gate)

Nikolai Bulykin's aerial frontal photograph shows the outer horses turning away from the middle pair. The wings form a tall open V above the head. The goddess holds her wreath out to her right, with the palm rising beside the opposite shoulder. These details informed the separate feathers, robes, wreath, palm and two open spoked wheels in the Blender mesh. [Frontal photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Санкт-Петербург,_Нарвские_ворота,_колесница.jpg)

Both playable pages use the gate at **−4329.2, 3510.4**. The shared model includes the column flutes, acanthus capitals, warriors and gilded inscriptions of the Narvsky scene. The Blender figure stands 23.7 m above street level and uses one patinated material with an 850 m draw distance. Rebuild the 4,684 triangles with `blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -P tools/model-narva-victory.py`.

## New Holland

The seasonal rink replaces the island's main lawn. The operator gives its area as over 2,000 m² and describes the pavilion where visitors change skates. An earlier winter programme records hundreds of overhead bulbs. The press-service photograph shows their spacing and the timber board finish. [Operator's rink page](https://www.newhollandsp.ru/rink/) · [Operator's winter programme](https://www.newhollandsp.ru/upload/iblock/9e8/9e8c2e277554e9f9e0589556e63619f6.pdf) · [Press-service photograph](https://www.metronews.ru/partners/press/reviews/novogodnie-prazdniki-v-novoy-gollandii-2184280/)

The Kolomna and Admiralty scenes both contain the same mapped ice polygon, OSM way `1011443504`, centred at **−3425.1, 268.4**. The ice and its supporting floor share the same triangles, 0.14 m above the city datum. Six rows of lamps span the rink. Two six-metre entrances interrupt the timber boards on opposite sides. Each board and mast has an individual breakable collider. The boards finish at 250 m, the masts and strings at 265 m and the ice at 480 m.

## Squares

The square installations are seasonal interpretations using the game's Petersburg firs, arches and ornaments. The surveyed open paved ground sets their positions. The established Teatralnaya and Baltic forecourt installations remain in their shared world positions.

| Site | World X, Z of the fir | Installation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stachek Square | −4307, 3544 | 15 m fir southeast of the gate, two illuminated arches, globes and snowman |
| Kulibina Square | −3835, 1250 | 11 m fir, walk-through arch, globes, snowman and a timber market stall |
| Repina Square | −4026, 1687 | 13 m fir, pair of walk-through arches, globes and a timber market stall |

The Mariinsky garden plots, Nikolsky Garden and the New Holland lawn use fresh snow on planting and compacted snow on gravel. The two granite frog fountains retain their sculpted rims and frogs. Their winter covers have walkable surfaces.
