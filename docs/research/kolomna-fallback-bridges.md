# Six Kolomna crossings: visual companions

The separate `landmarksSouthernCrossings.js` module supplements the existing
fallback floors and OSM roads; it is registered in Kolomna. It draws
low side members and breakable rails, keeping the existing deck, bank openings,
water and boat clearance. The footprints follow the currently implemented
bridge zones, not a surveyed reconstruction. Do not remove those fallback
zones while this module uses them.

Sources are the bridge operator's cards for [Матисов](https://mostotrest-spb.ru/bridges/matisov),
[Банный](https://mostotrest-spb.ru/bridges/bannyj),
[Бумажный](https://mostotrest-spb.ru/bridges/bumazhnyj),
[Молвинский](https://mostotrest-spb.ru/bridges/molvinskij),
[Екатерингофский](https://www.mostotrest-spb.ru/bridges/ekateringofskij), and
[Гутуевский](https://mostotrest-spb.ru/bridges/gutuevskij).

| Bridge | Published description | Representation and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Матисов | 39.7 × 10.5 m; three-span metal arches, Moika-pattern cast iron rails, granite supports | Moika-pattern rail and shallow metal arched faces; the intermediate supports are omitted. |
| Банный | 39 × 15.3 m; single-span concrete frame, original moving-pattern metal rail | pale flat concrete faces and a hairpin-pattern approximation to its rail. |
| Бумажный | 24 × 20 m; three-span concrete beam, running-post cast rail, tram | pale flat faces and a hairpin approximation; piers and tram detail omitted. |
| Молвинский | 25 × 27 m overall; concrete span with granite arch-like faces, simple metal rail | granite-coloured shallow arch and simple rods. |
| Екатерингофский | 66.8 × 21.95 m; single-span steel, Art Nouveau rail, granite abutments | steel faces with an Art Nouveau pattern approximation; no abutment detail. |
| Гутуевский | 95.7 × 28.2 m; three-span concrete beam, simple welded rail, granite supports | shallow concrete faces and simple rods; supports omitted. |

The open-water spans are sampled against the area's OSM water polygons; the
fallback floor widths can differ from the documented bridge widths. Molvinsky's
rail uses the documented 27 m width because the fallback floor's 20 m width
places it on the mapped carriageway; its existing walk floor remains 20 m wide.
The omitted
intermediate supports are deliberate until their mapped positions and boat
passages have been checked. Rail motifs are existing approximations and are not
claims of exact ornament. Integrated road-furniture, knock and full boat-passage
checks passed. Matisov was also inspected in the normal-loaded scene; visual
and vehicle checks on both approaches of each bridge remain useful follow-up.
