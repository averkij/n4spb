# Winter in Kronverk, Vvedensky and Petrovsky

## Cathedral Square and the island forecourts

The State Museum of the History of St Petersburg held its 2025–26 Christmas fair beneath a tree on Cathedral Square. In the scene, three timber stalls face the tree on the cobbled court west of the cathedral. The two light arches mark the approach from the south. The cathedral doors, the Boathouse and the main alley remain accessible. The stall and arch positions are an interpretation of the museum programme. [City cultural calendar](https://www.spbcult.ru/news/anonsy/petropavlovskaya-krepost-priglashaet-na-novogodnie-programmy/)

The Baltic House Christmas programme features family performances and a decorated tree inside the theatre. The outdoor arches on its paved approach are an original extension of that theme. On Petrovskaya Square the smaller tree and two illuminated baubles follow the public pavement around the southeastern bend. [Baltic House programme](https://baltic-house.ru/novosti/?ELEMENT_ID=2605)

## Cast eagle on the Peter Gate

François Vassou cast the lead eagle for the Peter Gate. Alexander Zakharov and Ivan Uvarov finished the dark figure and gilded the crowns, sceptre, orb and shield details. The frontal photograph shows the gaps between the crown plates, upturned wing tips, hooked beaks, curled tail feathers and the mounted figure in the red escutcheon. The sceptre is to the viewer’s left and the orb to the right. [Petrograd District Libraries](https://dates.pr-cbs.ru/events/na-petrovskie-vorota-petropavlovskoj-kreposti-vodruzhen-gerb-rossii-dvuglavyj-oryol/) · [Frontal photograph of the cast, 2022](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Орёл_Петровских_ворот_Петропавловской_крепости_2022.jpg)

The model uses shallow sculpted layers against the stone, with the silver horse and rider standing proud of the shield. At game scale it spans 3.51 m. The Blender script exports one vertex-coloured mesh for both seasons. [Model source](../../tools/model-petrovsky-eagle.py)
