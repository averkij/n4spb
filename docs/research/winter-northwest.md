# Winter on Yelagin Island, in Park 300 and around Dolgoye Lake

## Yelagin Island

The park floods its Great Square and puts the New Year tree in the middle. Its map locates the rink beside the open-air stage. In the game, the ice follows OSM pitch `r19991039`, with the fir at (−4740, −5388). The outside boards have two public entrances. The booths and lighting use the park's winter programme as a reference. Old Theatre Square and Zenit Square have separate arrangements for the game. [Park and rink](https://gutspkoimk.kkult.gov.spb.ru/central-park/) · [Park map](https://elaginpark.org/map/)

Four Carrara marble vases from the Triscorni workshop stand on the palace's east stair. The museum's conservation photograph shows fluting, figures in low relief and mask handles. The museum covers the sculptures with protective boxes in late autumn. The two seasons show the carvings and the winter enclosures respectively. [Museum photograph and winter conservation](https://elaginpark.org/news/dekorativnye-vazy-na-vostochnoy-lestnitse-elaginoostrovskogo-dvortsa-gotovyat-k-zime-/) · [Palace inventory](https://gutspkoimk.kkult.gov.spb.ru/central-park/dvortsovo-parkovyy-ansambl/elaginoostrovskiy-dvorets/)

## Park 300 and Begovaya

The lighthouse at the waterfront in the Park of the 300th Anniversary has a three-stage granite shaft, slotted cornices and a double rim at the top. The fountain has a long dry island. The holiday square lies on the open apron towards the sea, preserving the basin perimeter. Smaller installations mark the pedestrian approach at Begovaya and the waterfront at Piterland. Their distribution is a seasonal interpretation. [Stone contractor](https://vozrtd.ru/portfolio/park-300-letiya-sankt-peterburga/) · [Column photograph](https://peterburg.center/sites/default/files/4._park_of_300_years_spb._fountain._2015.jpg)

## Komendantsky Aerodrom and Dolgoye Lake

The district holds a New Year concert near Olkhovaya Street and Korolyova Avenue. The game places the tree, market and stage lights around the surveyed park entrance. The pedestrian area by Komendantskaya Square and the edge of Seleznyov Garden have smaller installations. [City winter programme](https://visit-petersburg.ru/event/novyy-god-v-parke-ozero-dolgoe/)

The paired memorial biplanes in Seleznyov Garden refer to the Polikarpov U-2. In the frontal photograph each has a two-blade propeller with rounded, tapered ends, ahead of the blue fuselage and crossed wing bracing. The game retains the photographed placement on two inclined pedestals.
