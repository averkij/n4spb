Both pavilions have separate near detail and longer range shell batches, so the 1.4 km between them does not keep the other's detail in view.

## Reference evidence

- [Petersburg Metro vestibule address register, 26 December 2025](https://metro.spb.ru/uploads/document/adress_vestibule_26.12.2025.pdf): Автово at пр. Стачек, 90 корп. 2 лит. А, Кировский завод at пр. Стачек, 70 лит. А.
- [Petersburg Metro history project, first stations](https://www.historystationspb.ru/epohimetro1): both opened on **15 November 1955**; Автово architects E. A. Levinson and A. A. Grushke; Kirovsky Zavod pavilion designed by A. K. Andreev and described as an ancient temple likeness with **44 Doric columns**. The 44 count refers to the **surface pavilion**, not the underground platform.
- [Candela's architectural lighting account for Автово](https://candela.ru/works/arkhitekturnaya-podsvetka/stantsiya-metro-avtovo/): a **double six-column portico**, dome on a large light drum, military ornament at the front roof corners and lettering on the Stachek-facing cornice. This directly governs the portico rows, roof silhouettes and dome.
- [Wikimedia Commons exterior photograph of Автово](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Metro_SPB_Line1_Avtovo_pavilion.jpg): ochre stucco, fluted columns, five dark arched bays, fascia rosettes and station name, wide grey steps, pale metallic dome and dark green roof sculptures. Visually inspected; the photo is a reference, not a shipped texture.
- [Petersburg Metro 2020 annual report, PDF page 5](https://metro.spb.ru/uploads/document/METRO_GO_2020_A4_v38.pdf): elevated view reveals the round upper windowed colonnade, shallow metal roof apron and blue-grey sheet-metal dome behind the portico. This official image corrected the first-pass solid drum. Visually inspected; no image is shipped.
- [Archi.ru exterior photograph of Кировский завод](https://archi.ru/projects/russia/13133/stanciya-metro-kirovskii-zavod): square ochre temple-like mass, deep colonnade, granite platform and steps, low flat roof. Visually inspected; no photo asset is shipped.
- [Kirovsky Zavod heritage protection record](https://base.garant.ru/403137267/53f89421bbdaf741eb2d1ecc4ddb4c33/): granite steps, Doric fluted columns, entablature and decorated architectural treatment.
- [Санкт-Петербургские ведомости, 3 February 2026](https://spbvedomosti.ru/news/gorod/70-let-spustya-pustaya-nisha-vestibyulya-stantsii-kirovskiy-zavod-obrela-byust-lenina/): the bronze Lenin bust from the platform was placed in **December 2025** in the semicircular niche of the Stachek-facing main facade, on a new pedestal. OSM has no bust point here. Its model position is therefore approximate within that documented central bay.

## Modeling and verification notes

The Avtovo frame faces west (local facade yaw `-1.3963` radians), with the dome behind the portico to the east. Kirovsky Zavod's NW entrance wall has local yaw `-2.0178` radians. The 44 perimeter columns use twelve positions on each of four sides, counting the corners once. Column fluting is geometry, rather than a flat texture. The metro signs, arch faces, cornices, drum windows and roof trophies are secondary detail at 430 m LOD; main masses remain visible to 1350 m. No station platform or escalator is claimed by this surface module.

`node tools/check.mjs landmarksAvtovoMetro` passes with **20,688 triangles, 30 meshes, 75 colliders** (including individual step surfaces), no update callbacks and zero nonfinite vertices. Integrated `tools/shot.mjs --area avtovo --only environment,streets,city,landmarksAvtovoMetro` rendered both pavilions with their mapped neighboring buildings and Stachek road. The across-street Avtovo frame shows the dome above its cornice; the elevated frame shows the windowed drum and now fully metal roof apron; Kirov's front and close niche views show the column ring, granite steps, frieze and bust. Four final frames plus one roof verification frame are in git-ignored `shots/landmarksAvtovoMetro/`. The shown scene was a context subset, so these frames do not establish full-area FPS or shipping-bake performance; root's integrated bake and claim/road audits remain the final gate.
