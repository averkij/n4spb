# Famous graves of the Smolensky Orthodox Cemetery (Vasilyevsky, area `vasilyevsky`)

Code: `src/world/landmarksSmolensky/famous.js` (built before the grounds; claim key `smolensky-grounds`). Round of 2026-10-29, branch `fx-monu`.
Rule used: a person is authored only when the burial place is confirmed by at least two sources AND the look and the rough position are known; positions are along the real alleys
of the module (`landmarksSmolensky/place.js` alley geometry) or on the OSM memorial node of the grave. Anything else is listed under "not authored" with the reason.

Street frame: x east, z south, yaw 0 = north. "Monument frame": local +z looks at the nearest alley, the head of the grave is at -z.

## Knock policy of the new stones (the Admiralty rule)
Stone tablets, steles, crosses and the heart pedestal are STRUCTURES (solid colliders, like the module's other granite memorials and the Finland Regiment obelisk); every monument stands at least
1.2 m off the alley edge (`famous.js spotBeside`: gap to the alley edge > r + 0.5), so no collider crosses a footpath. The Sologub plot fence is 6 breakable bays (`lib/fence-bays.js`, anchored,
metal). Grave lamps, flower pots and the toy hedgehog are knock records (`lib/knock-furniture.js`, kind `pot`). Water: none of the spots is near water (the Smolenka bank is > 150 m away).

## Ground clearing
`famous.js` runs before `grounds.js` and registers a disc (`[x, z, r, id]`, r 2.2-2.4 m) for each monument in the shared plan (`place.js plan()`): `place2.js planPlots` and `inZone` then place no
grave of the generic field inside it, and the trees / shrubs of the plan inside r + 1.4 m are removed (6 trunks in all). No file of the ground / furniture modules was edited.

## Not authored (and why)
- **Alexander Mozhaysky** (1825-1890), the aircraft pioneer: burial at the Smolensky cemetery confirmed (Wikipedia ru, the state list of graves) but the sources disagree on the look ("white metal cross and fence" / "a stele with a flower bed in a high natural-stone kerb with a metal fence") and give no alley or plot. Not placed.
- **Lidia Charskaya** (1875-1937): the cemetery lists a grave, other sources say she lies at Adler (the St Petersburg one a cenotaph); disputed, not authored.
- **A. A. Friedmann, N. N. Zinin, P. P. Semenov-Tyan-Shansky, V. Ya. Bunyakovsky, V. E. Makovsky, Yu. Yu. Klever, N. P. Likhachev** and the other names of the state list (Wikipedia ru "Перечень памятников, охраняемых государством"): burial confirmed, but neither the look nor the position found in two sources (Semenov-Tyan-Shansky: "the present monument of 1958", nothing more).
- **Arina Rodionovna**: only a memorial plaque at the entrance (the grave is unknown); it is not a grave, no position given beyond "at the entrance".
- **Blok's other site, the Literatorskie mostki** (Volkovo): outside this area.
- **Euler** (reburied in the 1950s at the Lazarevskoe cemetery of the Alexander Nevsky Lavra), the famous "German's gazebo" over the grave of General A. von Beckmann (the film "Brother", 1997) and the other names of the **Smolensky Lutheran cemetery** (Dokuchaev, Nobel, Greig, Nesselrode, Baird), and the **Smolensky Armenian cemetery** (Avsharov, Takhtagyan, Khasatov): these two cemeteries are separate OSM polygons (r7738342 Lutheran, r7738341 Armenian) OUTSIDE the Orthodox polygon that the module draws and have no authored grounds (alleys, graves) in the game yet. A follow-up round could author the Lutheran gazebo and the Armenian church with their grounds.
