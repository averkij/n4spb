# Area preloader facts

Every live area has exactly twenty short Russian sentences in
`assets/splash/<area>/facts.json`: 420 entries across 21 areas. The style follows
the original central preloader: concise, factual sentences about local history,
architecture, names and city geography, without game instructions or sales copy.
Neighbouring landmarks can recur where the implemented territories overlap.

One fact is selected at random for each load. The image selected by the inline
startup is preserved, so fetching metadata does not flash a second photograph.
Neither fact nor photograph rotates during that load. Facts and image metadata
load independently: a failed request leaves the other content and loading
controls usable. The old inline facts remain a network-failure fallback only.

Headings use `src/menu/area-labels.js`, generated by `node tools/menu-data.mjs`
from the same catalogue as the menu. They contain only the clean visible name,
such as `Васька 1`, with no city prefix. The kicker is hidden before startup and
removed from the live card. The initial technical heading and legacy fallback
fact stay hidden until their final content is ready.

## Content and references

These are original short summaries, not quotations. Sources were consulted on
2026-11-14. Prefer stable historical facts over visitor schedules, commercial
claims and changing superlatives. Dates in conflicting sources were omitted
unless a responsible institution supplied clear evidence. For example, D-2 was
launched in 1929 and commissioned in 1931; no conflicting flagpole height is used.

## Verification

`npm run preloader:test` checks all 21 actual preloader pages, data length and
uniqueness, clean names shared with the menu, removal of the kicker, all twenty
random fact choices and all five image choices, and stability beyond the old
rotation intervals. It also checks each metadata-request failure independently
and the transformed Pages mobile entries. WebGL startup is held at the module
boundary to inspect the preloader without building twenty-one scenes.

Screenshots were inspected in Edge at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, including the long
`Выборгская сторона` heading. Existing preloader typography, photography, progress
controls and mobile notes remain. No new visual design was imposed on the scene
preloaders. The facts and labels are runtime presentation data and do not change
baked geometry; the bake plan reports all 21 live areas fresh.
