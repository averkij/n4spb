# intro-draft.html: how it is built

`maps/intro-draft.html` is a single self-contained page (data embedded, no network): a draft of the START MAP of the app, where a visitor picks the part of Saint Petersburg to walk in.

* **Areas** = the 111 real municipal districts (`admin_level=8`, the "okrugs") of the federal city and its towns, after the manual merges and the carve of `edits.json` (104 areas), coloured by the 18 real city districts (`admin_level=5`). Their borders follow streets, rivers and canals (OpenStreetMap).
* **Size classes** against area B (the North, 3.947 km2): `b` = B +-20 % (3.16-4.74 km2), `large` = up to 1.5 x B (can stay), `big` = more (to split, `split` = about area / B), `small` = less than B -20 % (to merge; `mergeGroup` pairs small neighbours so the sum is close to B).
* Land area is rasterised (40 m cells) from the OSM polygons minus the sea (coastline) and inland water.
* The LIVE areas come from `src/areas/registry.js` (`build-intro.mjs`: every row with a `mapId` that is not `parked`; the badge letters are the `LIVE_LETTER` table there): P Palace-Liteyny (98), S Smolninskoye (96), A Admiralty-Haymarket (46), V Vladimirsky-Ligovka (97), W Vasilyevsky-Okrug 7 (48), G Gavan (49), M Morskoy (47), D Dekabristov Island (51), K Kolomna?Yekateringofsky (42), I Semyonovsky?Izmaylovsky (45). Each is drawn as dashed rectangles (the registry's `play` rectangles = the walkable territory), its district gets an orange outline, a `Live` badge in the list and a `Live scene` block on the card (sights, approxMB, walkable km2 as the union of the rectangles, the seamless-crossing neighbours from the edge gates). The parked strip and North (old A / B scenes) are no longer drawn; 'area B' (3.95 km2) stays only the size reference. A new live area = a registry row with `mapId` + one letter in `LIVE_LETTER`, then rebuild (`build-intro.mjs`, `build-streets.mjs`, `build-overlap.mjs`, `assemble.mjs`). The 'By source' mode and its counters describe the SOURCE project's coverage, not the live areas, and do not change with them.

## Rebuild

```
cd maps/intro-draft-src
node fetch.mjs            # (optional) the base layers of the earlier map: coast, districts, boundary, rivers, water, roads, c_* ; cached in ./raw
node fetch2.mjs           # municipal districts, places, main roads (Overpass); cached in ./raw
node fetch-tiles.mjs      # (optional) named secondary / tertiary streets for the outskirts, in tiles (the one-shot query times out)
node extract-source-coverage.mjs   # the playable rectangles + sights of the source project (coverage.html) -> raw/source-coverage.json
                          # the street names of the centre come from the source project's raw OSM (data/osm-raw/roads.json) when it is present
node build-intro.mjs      # -> intro-data.json (git-ignored)
node build-streets.mjs    # adds the named street chains, label anchors and each area's border streets to intro-data.json
node build-overlap.mjs    # the area overlap rules of docs/AREAS.md section 12: walkable territory (fp), overlap zones (ext), water (wz), shared junctions, bridges between areas
node assemble.mjs         # -> ../intro-draft.html
node check-edits.mjs      # (optional) the edits of edits.json did what they promise (merged areas present, live areas at their registry mapId, territory reach, the carve tiles without overlap or gap); exit 1 on a failure
node plot-areas.mjs 42,45,6,90 out.png   # (optional) a PNG of the listed areas (k) with Ligovsky prospekt (black) and the carve cut line (red dashed), through Edge
node shot-intro.mjs out "" 1500 900   # a PNG of the page through Edge (puppeteer-core from the repo's node_modules)
```

## Manual edits (edits.json)

The 111 districts are cut and merged by hand in `edits.json`; the page shows 104 areas.

* `merge`: `{ names, ru, en, stable? }` unions the districts `names` (plain names without "округ"; the FIRST one keeps the OSM id, the city district and the label point) into one area called `ru` / `en`. By default the removed districts shift the index `k` of every later area down by one; `"stable": true` fills the freed slot with the LAST district instead, so the `k` of all other areas stays (the registry `mapId` of the live areas, `docs/AREAS.md`, `tools/area-shape.mjs --k` depend on it; any merge that frees a slot below a live area needs it). Done: Дворцовый–Литейный, Владимирский–Лиговка, Адмиралтейский–Сенной (all live) and **Васильевский–Округ № 7 / Vasilyevsky–Okrug № 7** (5.76 km2, `large`; stable: Коломяги moved from 106 to 50). The new area is a Василеостровский район area (district of the first name; OSM puts Округ № 7 itself into the Адмиралтейский район, so that district goes from 6 to 5 areas and 17.0 to 13.5 km2).
* `cut`: `{ street, end?, to?, maxM? }`, read by `build-overlap.mjs`. It closes the gap between the END of a named street (the chain end that lies furthest in the compass direction `end`: n, s, e, w, ne, nw, se, sw; every end when omitted) and the nearest water cell within `maxM` (default 300 m; `to` may also be a `[lon, lat]` point) with a barrier line, as if the street ran on to the coast. The block faces of the overlap build (`R2`) are the faces of the street network; where a street stops short of the shore the two sides are ONE face, owned by whichever district has most of its cells, and every neighbour of that face reaches 320 m into the whole of it. Done: Большой проспект В.О., `end: sw`, 200 m to the Gulf shore. Before, the shore strip of Gavan west of it was one face with the south-west tip of Okrug 7 and the territory of Okrug 7 (the red line on hover) ran 1.75 km west along the harbour as a ragged tentacle; now the merged area reaches at most 370 m beyond its land. The land cells (the district rasters) are not touched by a cut, so no area changes its km2.

* `fillFootprintHoles`: `{ area, bbox: [west, south, east, north] }`, applied to the generated walkable footprint by `build-overlap.mjs`. Enclosed pockets whose cell centroid lies in the box gain their land cells; water remains in the water layer and municipal ownership is unchanged. This fills the southern enclosed pockets of Коломна–Екатерингофский without extending into open-ended neighbouring territory.

* `carve`: `{ from: [district names], into: <ru name of an area made by a merge>, street, side: "west" | "east", expectOuter? }`, applied by `build-intro.mjs` right after the merges (before the districts are rasterised, so every later stage sees the new rings): the part of each district in `from` that lies on `side` of the named street goes to the area `into`; the rest of the district stays an area of its own, with its k, and shares the new edge. The cut line is the CENTERLINE of the street: its ways are collected from `raw/{main_roads,secondary,tertiary}.json` (the dual carriageways), sampled every 10 m and binned by latitude (25 m), the longitude of a bin is the mean of its samples, north to south (the build asserts that every way is monotone in latitude, that the bins have no gap and that no bin mixes ways more than 150 m apart, and reports the numbers). Both ends are extended along the bearing of their last 800 m for 40 km, so the line runs through the whole of every district involved; the half-plane on `side` is that line plus two corners 1 degree beyond. The polygon work (intersection, difference, union, in full double precision, no snapping) is done by `carve.py` (Python 3 + `shapely` >= 2, `pip install shapely`; `build-intro.mjs` calls it through `child_process` with JSON on stdin / stdout, `N4_PYTHON` names the interpreter) and the build FAILS if python or shapely is missing, if the target union gets a new hole or has not `expectOuter` outer rings, or if the pieces do not tile exactly (overlap, gap, extra under 1e-6 km2). The numbers and the cut edge go to `intro-data.json` as `edits.carve` (for `check-edits.mjs` and `plot-areas.mjs`). Done: **Лиговский проспект**, west side: the north-west parts of Московская застава (6: 9.98 -> 6.81 km2, 3.16 km2 carved) and Волковское (90: 11.34 -> 10.53 km2, 0.82 km2 carved) join Семёновский–Измайловский.
* Done, the two merges after Vasilyevsky (both `stable`): **Коломна–Екатерингофский / Kolomna–Yekateringofsky** (k = 42, 4.97 km2, `large`; Екатерингофский freed k = 43 and Юнтолово moved there from 105) and **Семёновский–Измайловский / Semyonovsky–Izmaylovsky** (k = 45, 8.61 km2 with the carve, `big`, about 2 x B; Измайловское freed k = 44 and Лахта-Ольгино moved there from 104). The registry mapIds of the live areas (46 47 48 49 51 96 97 98) do not move.

Note for worktrees: `build-intro.mjs` WRITES `raw/grid-water.bin`, `raw/grid-lab.bin` and `raw/grid.json` (the rasters `build-overlap.mjs` reads), so a worktree needs its own copy of `raw/`, not a junction to another checkout's.

`raw/` and `intro-data.json` are git-ignored (about 25 MB of Overpass responses). Data: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## Source project coverage (D:/git2/agi-fun-tests/102_3d_piter)

The source scene plays in 76 rectangles (~86 km2 incl. water) and has 411 authored sights. The page shows them as green hatching, a colour mode ('By source') and a card line per area: 19 of the 111 areas are covered >= 90 % (the whole historic core), 5 are partial, 13 are touched at the edge, 74 have nothing. Its raw OSM (`data/osm-raw/roads.json`, box lon 30.179-30.415, lat 59.844-59.988) supplies every named street of the centre.
