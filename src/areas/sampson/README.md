## Reference anchors

- [Cathedral official address](https://sampsony.ru/contacts/): Bolshoy Sampsoniyevsky Prospekt 41. [Visit Petersburg](https://visit-petersburg.ru/leisure/places/tp-195636-sampsonievskiy-sobor/) describes the blue church, bell tower, chapel and garden on the former cemetery. [Wikimedia exterior photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Spb_06-2012_Sampsonievsky_Church.jpg), [church facade photograph](https://sobory.ru/photo/433587), [bell tower photograph](https://sobory.ru/photo/433586), and [chapel photograph](https://sobory.ru/photo/433584) show the blue and white arcades, five dark domes, open belfry arches, tented spire, and the chapel's ornate lantern. The authored church has an open portico with recessed arched windows; the gateway, belfry and chapel use the mapped blue and white architectural details. OSM building parts locate the 45 m nave at about (−463, −4010), the bell tower at (−509, −4001), and the chapel at (−496, −4034); the tower's mapped building height is 37.5 m. The landmark module claims the parent buildings and their parts to prevent duplicate generic massing.
- [City transport department](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/tr_infr_kom/news/319525/) identifies Bolshoy Sampsoniyevsky Prospekt as the Vyborg Side axis between Pirogovskaya Embankment and Engels Prospekt. Its route and side streets come from OSM, including the mapped railway and metro entrances.
- [Sampsoniyevsky Bridge](https://mostotrest-spb.krti.gov.spb.ru/bridges/sampsonievskij) and [Grenadersky Bridge](https://mostotrest-spb.krti.gov.spb.ru/bridges/grenaderskij) are the two authored bridge groups; both span the navigable Bolshaya Nevka. The bridge geometry is reused from the current game's detailed source modules.
- [Official metro map](https://metro.spb.ru/map1/route.html) confirms Vyborgskaya and Lesnaya. OSM places the public spaces at the following local positions: the western part of Sampsoniyevsky Garden around (−450, −4080), Stroganovsky Park (−2506, −5670), Golovinsky Garden (−1721, −5423), and Lanskaya further north. The cathedral and garden views are inside the mapped park, away from the carriageways; the other views are placed on safe public ground near these mapped locations.

The OSM data has current building footprints, levels, roof tags, streets, crossings, tram tracks, parks, trees, water and street furniture. The four default station cars remain elsewhere; this area starts with one purple Zhiguli. Yelagin pond records at the western overlap use the shared Neva datum, as in Okrug 65, to avoid water-level steps.
